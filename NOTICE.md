## Original game content

*Jumping Jim* — its design, graphics, music, sound effects and the original
ZX Spectrum Next binary (`original/`), together with the data extracted from it
(`assets/`) — was created by **roman126**:
<https://roman126.itch.io/jumping-jim-zx-spectrum-next>.
It is included and ported **with the author's permission**. Please respect that
authorship when redistributing it, and credit roman126.

*Jumping Jim* is itself an AI-assisted reimagining of *Jumping Jack*
(Imagine Software, 1983).

The Retro Informática Paraguay logo (`assets/branding/rip_logo.png`) belongs to
Retro Informática Paraguay.

The level backgrounds (`assets/backgrounds/`) are not part of the original game: they were
made for this port (drawn by `tools/backgrounds/sketch_backgrounds.py`) and are covered by
the MIT License like the code.

## AI assistance

This port was developed with the assistance of artificial intelligence
